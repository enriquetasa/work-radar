Work Radar keeps your projects on your desktop and syncs them between your devices.

Choose the download for your computer:

- macOS: `mac-arm64.dmg` for Apple Silicon, or `mac-x64.dmg` for Intel.
- Windows: `windows-x64-setup.exe` to install, or `windows-x64-portable.exe` to run without installing.
- Linux: `linux-x64.AppImage` (mark the file executable before opening).

Open the app and sign in with your invited email address. Open the magic link on the same computer while Work Radar remains open. You can also use the app offline without signing in. Each account has its own radar.

The Windows portable download still stores data in your OS account's app-data folder. Sign in again on another computer to sync; copying the executable does not copy your data or login.

Automatic updates are not included in this release. Download and install a newer version to upgrade; your app-data folder is retained. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Before publishing this draft, the maintainer must confirm the hosted database and email configuration, verify signing/notarization, and complete the fresh-install and upgrade checks in `docs/distribution.md`. Unsigned builds may show operating system warnings.
