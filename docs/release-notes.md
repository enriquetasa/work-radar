Work Radar v0.1 brings project tracking, recovery history, attachments, and optional sync into one desktop app.

## Highlights

- Keep projects organized with priorities, statuses, notes, and review schedules.
- Create and edit projects in a consistent full-width form, with quick review-date choices for tomorrow, in 3 days, in a week, or in 2 weeks.
- Choose categories from a dropdown and manage its options in Settings. Dropdown choices are saved per profile on this device and included in backups.
- Compare saved project versions, restore previous edits, and recover deleted projects.
- Add project attachments and export or import full backups containing their files.
- Start locally or sign in with an invited account. Accounts use separate local profiles, with optional Supabase sync.
- Opt in to morning briefings when the backend delivery service is configured.

## Downloads

Choose the download for your computer:

- macOS: `mac-arm64.dmg` for Apple Silicon, or `mac-x64.dmg` for Intel. ZIP downloads are also available.
- Windows: `windows-x64-setup.exe` to install, or `windows-x64-portable.exe` to run without installing.
- Linux: `linux-x64.AppImage` (mark the file executable before opening).

Open the app and sign in with your invited email address. Open the magic link on the same computer while Work Radar remains open. You can also use the app offline without signing in.

The Windows portable download still stores data in your OS account's app-data folder. Copying the executable does not copy your data or login. Windows downloads are unsigned and may show an operating-system warning.

Automatic updates are not included. Install a newer version manually to upgrade; your app-data folder is retained. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
